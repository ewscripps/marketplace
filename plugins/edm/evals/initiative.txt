version: 1.0.0
prefix: TSVE
product: eval
description: tiny-svc-hardening

Harden the tiny-svc webhook relay (plugins/edm/evals/fixtures/tiny-svc/) so it is safe to
operate in production. Today tiny-svc's webhook endpoint accepts unauthenticated POSTs,
stores a downstream billing provider's API key in a plaintext config file, retries a
failed downstream billing call zero times with no dead-letter path, performs no schema
validation on the incoming payload, emits no logs or metrics on the worker's downstream
call path, and ships with no automated test suite. Produce a scope, an SRD, and an SRD
audit that close these gaps without changing tiny-svc's externally observed behavior for
valid, authenticated traffic. Treat tiny-svc as the entire system under study -- there is
no other service, database, or external dependency to consider beyond what is in the
fixture tree.
