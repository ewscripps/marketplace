### Independent verification of the EDMV4-T50 / T51 portability claims

Recorded because the task asked for it explicitly, not as findings:

- **T50 (bash-4 constructs): claim holds.** A tree-wide sweep for `declare -A`, `local -A`,
  `mapfile`, `readarray`, `${v^^}`, `${v,,}`, `{fd}`, `coproc`, `wait -n`, `&>>`, `|&`, brace
  character ranges and `shopt -s lastpipe` returns only prose mentions, grep-pattern arguments and
  test fixtures. No real usage in `bin/` (test suites included), `evals/`, or the four new gate
  scripts. `${!FILES[@]}` in `edm-lint-artifacts` and `printf -v` in `bin/tests/` are bash 3.0/3.1,
  below the floor. Every `=~` right-hand side is unquoted, which is correct for bash 3.2+.
- **T51 (required-binary set): one real miss.** No `node`/`python`/`yq`/`ruby`/`deno` anywhere;
  `perl` correctly guarded in `timing.sh` -- but unguarded in `wave6-smoke.sh` (L8-013), which
  T51's own scope exclusion knowingly leaves open.
- **Unpaired GNU-only invocations elsewhere: one real miss.** `sed -i` appears only in
  `bin/tests/` in the portable attached-suffix form `sed -i.bak`; `date -d` appears only paired
  behind BSD `date -v` (`wave8-smoke.sh:4427`); `sort -V` appears once in `bin/tests/` on
  version-comparison output where BSD `sort` also accepts `-V` on current macOS; no `grep -P`
  outside the documented probe; no `readlink -f`; no `head -n -N`; every `mktemp` uses an explicit
  `${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/...XXXXXX` template with no trailing suffix and no bare `mktemp -d`. The one
  genuine unpaired divergence point is the BSD-only `stat -f` at `wave8-smoke.sh:2073` (L8-012),
  which both sweeps exclude by their shared `grep -v '/tests/'` filter.
- **`wave7-smoke.sh` T61 AC11's widened exemption is sound in shape**: the
  `grep -vE 'stat -c.*stat -f|stat -f.*stat -c'` filter exempts by line shape rather than by
  filename, and the positive control at :1241-1254 proves an unpaired `stat -c` still fails. Its
  residual gap is the `/tests/` exclusion at :1223, not the exemption itself.
