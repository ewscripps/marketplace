### Details

#### Finding L11-001: the CA-164 fix made the lens JSONL schema resolvable only by reference, and this round the reference resolved to nothing

- **What exists**: the launch template at `skills/code-audit/SKILL.md:199-214`. `:207` names two output artifacts ("write your raw report to `${OUTPUT_DIR}/lens-L{N}.md` **and** `${OUTPUT_DIR}/lens-L{N}.jsonl`"); `:208-210` is the CA-164 replacement bullet, a pointer: "emit the schema documented in your own agent definition's `## JSONL Line Format` section"; `:213` closes with "and emit the same actionable/noted findings to your JSONL file using that schema."
- **What it's wired to**: first-hand evidence from this round's own spawn. The prompt delivered to L11 named exactly one output path, `.../pass-3_2026-08-08/lens-L11.md`. It carried no JSONL bullet, no schema pointer, and closed "Write your findings plus a 'Noted / Not Actionable' section to your assigned file" (singular). Separately, the agent definition delivered as the system prompt did **not** contain a `## JSONL Line Format` section, a `## Output` section naming two permitted write paths, or a `## Scope` section, and its Output Format table was headed `| # |` with a `| 1 |` example row and `#### Finding 1:` -- the pre-CA-165 shape. On-disk `agents/edm-audit-wiring.md` has all of those at `:78-86`, `:95-97` and `:114-139`.
- **What it should call**: the launch template as written. Both artifacts, and a schema the lens can reach without depending on a section of its own delivered prompt being present.
- **Break in the chain, and why it matters**: with no `lens-L11.jsonl`, the synthesizer's "Read the lens reports (prose and JSONL)" (`:232`) degrades to prose only, which is precisely the CA-020 break the JSONL contract was created to close; and `evals/score-artifacts.sh:414-417` skips dimension 5 entirely ("run does not include a code-audit round (no `lens-L*.jsonl` present)"), so the one metric that would have detected the loss reports `null` rather than a failure. Before CA-164 a lens carried a *wrong* schema; after CA-164 a lens whose delivered definition lacks the section carries *no* schema. The ledger already records the reasoning against exactly this trade in NOTED CA-128: the ~36-line skeleton is deliberately not factored to a single reference because "each lens runs in its own context with no guarantee it reads a referenced doc, the cost of a missed reference is a lost finding."
- **Fix**: (1) restore the literal one-line schema into the launch template *alongside* the pointer, matching CA-128's stated rationale -- the pointer alone is a single point of failure, and the two together cost one line; (2) land the step-8 precondition CA-176 already prescribes: refuse to spawn `edm-audit-synthesizer` until one `lens-L{N}.jsonl` exists for every member of `LENS_SET`, so a dropped JSONL half fails at the gate rather than surfacing an audit round later.
- **Filter note**: the stale delivered definition itself is a host/plugin-cache artifact of the same family as NOTED CA-130 and CA-181, recorded below. The repository-side change above is actionable independently of it, and is what makes the round robust to it.
